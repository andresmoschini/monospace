# Report a characterization instead of reviewing it

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0045](0045-pin-every-arrow-arrangement-as-a-reviewed-snapshot.md) pins 1,856 arrow
arrangements, 20,424 lines across eight files, and it was right about why: what a snapshot buys is
not correctness but a diff, so a change that moves a picture has to be looked at instead of passing
silently. It pinned them as a **reviewed** snapshot — the claim that the 1,856 were read once
against the model, which that record itself says "only a person can keep", alongside "a
reviewed-once artifact decays". The word turns an instrument of visibility into an obligation of
approval: a change to the ranking asks for a reading nobody can perform, so the safe answer becomes
not to change the ranking.

## Decision Drivers

- The question is not "did anything change?", which the test answers, but "did it change the way I
  wanted?".
- A claim nobody can keep is worse than no claim: it is believed by whoever reads it next, and here
  it was believed as a reason not to change the code.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **keep the sweep whole and drop the claim of review, requiring a report instead** —
because the sweep is what makes a change visible, and the report is what makes it legible.

Each time a characterization moves, what is produced is **how many cases moved, in which families of
arrangement, and three representative examples with their before and after**; that report is what is
read, and the file is not claimed to be. Testing also splits the two kinds apart: a contract test
pins a decision, so moving one belongs on the decision sheet, while a characterization records what
the code does over a range too wide to assert by hand.

### Consequences

- Good, because changing the ranking stops requiring the re-certification of 1,856 pictures.
- Good, because a reviewer can tell at a glance whether a diff is a decision or a derivation.
- Bad, because nothing in the gate checks that the report was produced, or that its three examples
  are representative.
- Neutral, because not one picture is removed, sampled or regenerated by this record.

## Reversibility

Cheap: it retires a sentence and adds a habit. What it does not undo is the sweep's real cost —
eight files move on any change to the ranking — which is paid by the test, where a reader meets it.

## Confidence

Medium-high (75%). What would prove it wrong: a defect landing through an accepted diff whose report
looked fine.
