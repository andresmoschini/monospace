## Notes

- **Every box is checked as of the `/speckit-clarify` session of 2026-09-10.** The two that were
  unchecked on purpose are the two that session was for.

- **On "no [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] markers remain".** Four markers stood, and all four are answered in
  _Clarifications_ in [spec.md](../spec.md): the minimum valid length of a straight line, whether a
  route may leave the rectangle its endpoints span, what an arrow does when both endpoints coincide,
  and which glyphs draw a line's ends and an arrow's heads. A fifth question was put and answered in
  the same session — where a two-bend route bends on an even span — which was carried as an
  assumption rather than as a marker.

- **On "user scenarios cover primary flows".** User Story 3 is now implementable: the direction
  families table has no row without a defined outcome. Three of its rows are pinned by pictures the
  maintainer supplied, two are settled by the general routing rule with the picture to be produced
  at implementation, and the same-position row is settled by not being pinned at all.

- **Two answers changed what the spec asks for rather than only filling a gap.** The maintainer
  chose to let degenerate arrangements fall out of the general rule instead of acquiring exceptions
  of their own, which rewrote FR-017 and FR-019 and softened SC-001 and SC-003; and chose to have
  the caller supply the end and head glyphs as a scope cut, which rewrote FR-027, added FR-028 and
  SC-010, and added an _Out of scope_ entry naming what was deferred. Neither is a decision this
  checklist can verify by reading the spec alone — both are recorded where the reasoning is.

- **On "no implementation details".** The spec names project artifacts — `cargo xtask check`, the
  sections of [`docs/model.md`](../../../docs/model.md), `docs/glyph-sets.md`, the frozen specs, the
  ADRs in force, and the existing `Buffer` and `stamp` it must not change. That is deliberate and
  matches the checklist of feature 028: in this repository they are the vocabulary the maintainer
  reads a spec in, and they are what makes a criterion checkable rather than a matter of taste. What
  the spec does not name is how any of it is built — the brief's forbidden list is honored in full,
  and _Handoff to the plan_ records what was left for `/speckit-plan` instead of being quietly
  settled.

- **On "requirements are testable".** FR-002, FR-004, FR-006 and FR-009 are constraints on the shape
  of the abstraction rather than on output, so they are checked by reading and by SC-005 and SC-007
  rather than by an assertion about text. That is the same standard the constitution applies to thin
  slices and separate structural commits: checked by reading, at plan time and at review time.

- **One thing this checklist still cannot mark either way.** No picture in the spec has been
  produced by running code, because none of it exists yet. SC-001 is what makes that a temporary
  state rather than an unverified claim: it requires each picture in an acceptance scenario to be
  regenerated by running before it is asserted, per _Claims are measured, not assumed_. The pictures
  marked illustrative under _Edge Cases_ are exempt, and are asserted by nothing.
