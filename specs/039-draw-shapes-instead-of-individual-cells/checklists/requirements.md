## Notes

- **The two unchecked items are unchecked on purpose, and neither is fixed by editing the spec.**

- **On "no [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] markers remain".** Four markers stand, and they were required to
  stand: the maintainer's brief listed the questions it wanted left open and said to mark them
  rather than decide them. Deciding any of them in this file would also break _Decisions recorded
  when taken_ and _The model owns the design_ — the arrow's behavior in the unsettled families is
  model prose, not a spec's to invent. The markers are the minimum count that covers the brief's
  list:
  1. the minimum valid length of a straight line (User Story 2, scenario 4);
  2. whether a route may leave the rectangle its endpoints span — one marker for three of the
     brief's questions, because the facing-away, identical-direction and perpendicular-facing-away
     families all need the same answer (_Edge Cases_);
  3. what an arrow does when both endpoints are at the same position (_Edge Cases_);
  4. which glyphs draw a line's ends and an arrow's heads (FR-027).

  `/speckit-clarify` is where they go, and the answers to 2 and 3 land in the model amendment that
  _What the model owes this feature_ names.

- **On "user scenarios cover primary flows".** They cover the three figures, but User Story 3 cannot
  be implemented from the spec as it stands: four of the seven rows of the direction families table
  have no expected outcome yet. Stories 1 and 2 are complete and independently implementable today.

- **On "no implementation details".** The spec names project artifacts — `cargo xtask check`, the
  sections of [`docs/model.md`](../../../docs/model.md), `docs/glyph-sets.md`, the frozen specs, the
  ADRs in force, and the existing `Buffer` and `stamp` it must not change. That is deliberate and
  matches the checklist of feature 028: in this repository they are the vocabulary the maintainer
  reads a spec in, and they are what makes a criterion checkable rather than a matter of taste. What
  the spec does not name is how any of it is built — the brief's forbidden list is honored in full,
  and _Handoff to the plan_ records what was left for `/speckit-plan` instead of being quietly
  settled.

- **On "requirements are testable".** FR-002, FR-004, FR-006 and FR-009 are constraints on the shape
  of the abstraction rather than on output, so they are checked by reading and by SC-005 and SC-007
  rather than by an assertion about text. That is the same standard the constitution applies to thin
  slices and separate structural commits: checked by reading, at plan time and at review time.

- **One thing this checklist cannot mark either way.** Every picture in the spec is a placeholder
  awaiting FR-027, and none has been produced by running code, because none of it exists yet. SC-001
  is what makes that a temporary state rather than an unverified claim: it requires each picture to
  be regenerated by running before it is asserted, per _Claims are measured, not assumed_.
